About Me

I always liked pictures, and taking pictures when I was younger; my parents gave me a small older portable camera that could fit in my pocket. I’d take pictures of anything“cool” I saw, so, basically everything. I am still facsinated by old photographs and pictures I see, in movies, or just find on the internet. Photojournalism is what I also am more so interested in. I intend to later on in my life, perhaps now knowing the basics, take photography seriously, or as another source for income depending upon what I do, and what my job would be. I as well, think it'd be great to go to other places, and take photos to remember those times.
